Facebook reactions are seven emoji responses (Like 👍, Love ❤️, Care 🥰, Haha 😂, Wow 😮, Sad 😢 and Angry 😡) that let people show a specific emotion toward a post instead of a plain Like. Reaction What it means 👍 Like General approval or acknowledgement ❤️ Love Strong appreciation. The person really connected with the post 🥰 Care Empathy and support, usually on personal or community posts 😂 Haha Found it funny.
