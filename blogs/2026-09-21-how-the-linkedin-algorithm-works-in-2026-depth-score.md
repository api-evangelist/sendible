The LinkedIn algorithm in 2026 ranks content primarily on Depth Score: how long and how meaningfully people engage with a post (dwell time, comment quality, saves), rather than how many quick reactions it gets. This is the biggest shift in how LinkedIn distributes content since it moved from chronological to algorithmic feeds and it changes what "good" LinkedIn content looks like.
