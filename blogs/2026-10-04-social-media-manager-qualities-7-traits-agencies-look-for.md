The best social media managers are innovative, creative, detail-oriented, strong copywriters, analytical, organised and passionate about social. In an agency, those traits matter even more: you're juggling several client brands, voices, approval chains and reporting deadlines at once, so organisation and attention to detail keep quality consistent across clients. Being active on social media in some way, shape, or form is essential for growing businesses.
