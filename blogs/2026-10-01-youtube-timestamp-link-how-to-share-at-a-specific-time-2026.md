To share a YouTube video at a specific time, pause where you want it to start, then: on desktop, right-click the video and choose Copy video URL at current time ; on iPhone or Android, tap Share and turn on the timestamp toggle. You can also add ?t= plus the time to the end of any YouTube link, for example youtu.be/abc123?t=90 .
